## Demo Flow

1. Open `https://chatbot.jilanov.com`.
2. Show the Bulgarian-first landing page and Jilanov branding.
3. Click "Try live assistant" and ask a Bulgarian support or pricing question.
4. Show a product question and product-card response.
5. Show product comparison with the compare button.
6. Submit "Book a demo" with the customer's real website and platform.
7. Open the admin lead inbox and show the created lead.
8. Open the action audit and show why the assistant answered, routed, or notified.
9. Explain safety limits: no invented order, invoice, warranty, payment, delivery, return, or checkout status.
10. Close with the pilot package and a concrete install date.

## Objection Handling

- "Is this just ChatGPT?" No. It uses approved knowledge first, creates leads and handoffs, sends notifications, and records action audit events.
- "Will it lie about orders or prices?" No. Sensitive operational facts must come from approved knowledge or connected systems. Otherwise it asks for contact details or routes to staff.
- "Can we edit answers?" Yes. Admin users manage knowledge without code.
- "Can it use our products?" Yes. The current product loop supports pasted CSV/JSON feeds and public feed URLs. Native store connectors are the next integration step.
- "Can it work in Bulgarian?" Yes. Bulgarian is the target-market default; English is supported through browser-language detection and manual switching.
- "How fast can we start?" A simple lead assistant can be installed in one afternoon after access, content, and contact email are ready.
